# Legal source accuracy
Human-authored navigators point to the official 2026 Florida Statutes (review date 2026-10-19).

- §720.303: governance and official records. Proper request and specific statutory exemptions matter.
- §720.3035: architectural authority must derive from covenants or authorized published standards. Do not equate standards with physical entry/easement authority.
- §720.305(2): particular fine and suspension safeguards including hearing-right notice (at least 14 days), hearing (within 90 days), disinterested 3-member committee, notice of findings (within 7 days), cure protections, and conditions on payment dates. These are not all-purpose initial violation deadlines.
- §720.311: different dispute categories, including exceptions. Election/recall excluded from presuit mediation; collections of monetary obligations excluded from the specified presuit mediation path.
- §720.317: requirements for qualifying electronic voting, not satisfied merely by an online poll.

No source proves a particular HOA violated the law without documents, notice evidence, factual analysis and applicable exceptions. Check current text at https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/Chapter720. This is not legal advice.
