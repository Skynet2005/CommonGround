# CommonGround — Product review and differentiation

Competitive context as of October 19, 2026: established HOA systems such as PayHOA and Condo Control offer payments, violations, owner portals, voting, documents and AI assistance already. Adding those categories is not unique. Sources: https://www.payhoa.com/features/management/ and https://www.condocontrol.com/markets/hoa-management/.

## Must build first (P1)

- Verified identities AND separately verified ownership, tenancy, agent authority and board role.
- Source-locked statutes, recorded covenants and versioned community guidelines.
- A complete factual ledger that distinguishes allegations, concessions, competing evidence and independently verified findings.
- Notices tied to statutory prerequisites and actual proof of service, with human-verified deadlines.
- Equal procedural treatment across comparable cases, with protected resident privacy.
- Independent reviewer selection, mandatory conflict disclosure and meaningful challenges.
- Encrypted evidence, legally aware retention, custody export and data portability.
- Public community charter plus an auditable independent oversight organization.
- Confidential mediation walls, never bulk-exported or ingested into AI.

## Operational expansion (P2)

- Board seats, terms, agendas, meetings, notices, recusal tracking and approved minutes.
- Vendor bids, conflict declarations, selection rationale, warranties and inspection signoffs.
- Maintenance requests with work orders, realistic service expectations and independent closure confirmation.
- Financial budget-to-actual transparency, reconciliation and append-only journal corrections.
- Interoperable imports from incumbent HOA software, permissioned APIs and open export standards.
- Accessible low-bandwidth and multilingual experience, plus assisted access.
- Privacy-preserving community metrics with cohort sizes and suppression.

## Do not rush (P3)

- Legally compliant electronic elections and recalls under Florida statute §720.317, requiring secret-ballot security and independent audits.
- Automated accounting/payment execution, which requires double-entry balancing, processor compliance and approvals.
- AI-produced legal conclusions; only source-grounded advisory outputs after evaluations and legal reviewer signoff.
- Automated demand letters, lien filings, foreclosure, fines or entry onto property. These require valid human authority and individualized legal review.

## Evaluation

Track valid source coverage, time to meaningful answer, verifiable notice delivery, independent appeals, errors corrected, resident and board trust, voluntary noncoercive settlements, data export completion, participation access and governance costs. A high settlement rate alone is not a fairness metric. Publish independent audit methodology and conflicts of interest.

Best positioning: a trust-and-accountability infrastructure layer that can integrate with existing managers, not a race to reproduce generic accounting dashboards. Stewardship should be nonprofit or structurally insulated from any association or vendor that may become a party to disputes.
