# Production release gates — NOT YET COMPLETE
Do not host real private resident evidence or represent this tool as a secure multi-user dispute authority without all relevant checks.

## Security
- Verified authentication, account and membership proof, role revocation, MFA for privileged roles.
- Server-enforced per-case and per-community authorization for every read/write, with adversarial cross-tenant tests.
- Hardened sessions, request origin/CSRF protection, signed expiring invitations and rate limits.
- Private encrypted object storage, authorization-gated access, malware scanning, quotas and safe key management.
- Independent append-only server audit retention and externally anchored integrity checkpoints.
- Accessibility review, backups, restore drills, data minimization and retention/deletion policy.
- Third-party penetration test and privacy review before public rollout.

## Governance and legal requirements
- Qualified Florida counsel review covering Chapters 720, 617, 468 and property/easement rights.
- Distinguish §720.305 fine hearing procedure from general covenant disputes.
- Election/recall disputes and money collections are not ordinary §720.311 presuit mediation cases.
- Human authority for penalties, property entry, court filings, binding conclusions and resolution.
- Conflict screening of independent neutral reviewers, with independent oversight and an appeals mechanism.
- Versioned statutory texts (effective date, retrieval date, subsection, amendment history).
- Source documents and user allegations are separated from verified facts and legal conclusions.
